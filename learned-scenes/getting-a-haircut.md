# Getting a Haircut | 在理发店剪头发

## Process Breakdown | 流程拆解

| Step | English | 中文 |
|------|---------|------|
| 1 | Walk in and check in | 进门报到的 |
| 2 | Discuss what you want | 沟通需求 |
| 3 | Confirm length/style | 确认长度/发型 |
| 4 | Haircut in progress | 剪发中 |
| 5 | Pay and leave | 付款离开 |

---

## English Dialogue | 英文对话

**Setting: You walk into a salon. A stylist greets you.**

**Stylist:** Hi! Do you have an appointment?  
**You:** No, do you have any openings?  

**Stylist:** Yeah, come on in. Have you been here before?  
**You:** No, it's my first time here.  

**Stylist:** Cool. What would you like today?  
**You:** Just a trim, please. Maybe take off an inch or so.  

**Stylist:** Sure. Do you want me to keep it the same style?  
**You:** Yes, please. Just a little shorter on the sides.  

**Stylist:** (finishing) How's that?  
**You:** Perfect, thanks.  

**Stylist:** That'll be $25.  
**You:** (pay) Keep the change.  
**Stylist:** Thanks! See you next time.

## Learning Notes | 学习注释

### Vocabulary & Phrases | 生词与词组

- **trim** - 修剪；剪短
- **take off** - 去掉；剪掉
- **inch** - 英寸（约2.54厘米）
- **sides** - 两侧；两边
- **Keep the change** - 不用找零了（小费表达）

### Core Patterns | 核心句型

- **"Just a trim, please."** ← 最简洁的需求表达
- **"Take off [an inch / a little]."** ← 指明要剪多少
- **"How's that?"** ← 剪完后问效果（最常用）
- **"Keep the change."** ← 给小费的自然说法

### Synonym Swap | 同义替换

- "Just a trim" ← most common | 最常用
- "Just a little cut" ← more casual | 更随意
- "I'd like a haircut" ← more formal | 更正式

### Cultural Tip | 文化小贴士

- 美国理发店通常需要预约（appointment），walk-in 也可以但可能等久一点
- "Keep the change" 是给 1-2 美元小费的常见方式
- 理发师喜欢聊天气、运动等轻松话题，不想聊可以简单回应 "Yeah" 或 "Cool"
